## Public-domain basis

**17 USC § 105.** NOAA NHC staff work product. NWS/NHC reports are authored by
federal employees as part of official duties — clean PD. No contractor-mixing concern.

## Codec-corpus character

- **Color satellite imagery on cover pages** (storm composites, IR/visible)
- **Track maps** with land/ocean boundaries, wind-swath polygons, time-step markers
- **Wind-radii tables** + intensity tables (dense tabular data with grid lines)
- **Storm-surge inundation maps** (color choropleth)
- **Plain bureaucratic prose** in body text + meteorology jargon
