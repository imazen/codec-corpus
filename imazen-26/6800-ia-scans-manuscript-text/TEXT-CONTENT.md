# What the text pages say — content & "aged-well" check

Read directly from the full-res scans (no OCR engine; read off crops). All selected text
pages are art / natural-science / horticulture / bibliographic content. **Nothing
problematic was selected.** Notes below, plus caveats about each work's *other* material.

## By source

- **Haeckel — German, Fraktur (6 pp).** Preface (*Vorwort*) is a lyrical ode to the beauty
  of microscopic marine life (radiolaria, diatoms, protists) revealed by 19th-c. microscopy.
  The rest are taxonomic plate captions (e.g. "Tafel 1 — *Phaeodaria*", phylum Protozoa →
  class Radiolaria). Pure biology. The "höhere/niedere Lebensformen" (higher/lower life-forms)
  wording is period taxonomy about *organisms*, not people.
- **Owen Jones — English (11 pp).** Title page; preface (design philosophy: understand *why*
  an ornament was appropriate to its context rather than copy styles blindly); list of plates;
  and "Egyptian Ornament" essays analysing lotus/papyrus plant-motifs. Foundational design theory.
- **Redouté — French (15 pp).** Rose-cultivar descriptions: Latin botanical diagnosis +
  breeder, date, garden history, growth habit (e.g. *Rosa Gallica* 'Van Eeden', raised at
  Haarlem, planted at Malmaison 1810). Horticulture.
- **Hokusai — Japanese woodblock (3 pp).** Preface + colophon + publisher's catalogue
  (Hokusai named as author 葛飾北斎筆; Edo/Nagoya booksellers; ads for other art manuals).
- **Shin-Bijutsukai — Japanese letterpress (1 p).** Colophon/masthead: 1902 (Meiji 35)
  publication data, price, Kyoto publisher 山田直三郎. Bibliographic.

## Caveats (the works' OTHER material — NOT selected here)

- **Haeckel the author** promoted scientific racism / social Darwinism in his *other* books
  (*Welträtsel*, *Anthropogenie*). None of that is in *Kunstformen der Natur*, which is about
  non-human organisms. These text pages are clean.
- **Owen Jones's book** contains a chapter "Ornament of Savage Tribes" (dated terminology) and
  its Plate 3. **No page from it was selected.** Avoid that chapter/plate if pulling more.
- Japanese: 2 of the 4 pages were read character-by-character (colophons); the other two are
  same-category front/back matter (preface, mixed text+vignette) — low risk, not fully translated.
